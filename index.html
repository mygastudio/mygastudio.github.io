Whatever your age, the moment you begin again is your youth. MYGA Studio makes stories of short but great lives. Our first game, "땃쥐의 일생 (Shrew Life)", follows a tiny shrew that lives just two and a half years — and every stage of it, from newborn to old age, is its youth.
